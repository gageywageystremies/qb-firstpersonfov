# qb-firstpersonfov
Introducing the First-Person FOV Adjuster — a completely free, open-source utility designed to elevate your shooting experience:

Full FOV Customization
Effortlessly tweak your entire field of view to suit your playstyle. Whether you prefer a wide peripheral view or a tight, zoomed-in sight picture, simply set your desired values and let the tool handle the rest.

Seamless First-Person Transitions
The adjuster automatically snaps you into first-person view the moment you draw your weapon, ensuring precise aiming without manual camera swaps. Once you holster or put the gun away, it smoothly returns you to your previous camera perspective.

Lightweight & Easy Integration
Built for simplicity, it requires minimal setup and has no impact on performance. Ideal for both modders and end users looking to enhance immersion.

Open-Source & Community-Driven
Hosted on a public repository under a permissive license, you’re free to explore, modify, and contribute. All source code and documentation are available for review and collaboration.
